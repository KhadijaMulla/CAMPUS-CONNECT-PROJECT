How CampusConnect works

Three steps from browsing to being hired.

1. Set up your profile

Add your college, branch, skills and resume once. We use it to suggest opportunities that fit you.

2. Discover what is open

Search and filter internships, hackathons, workshops and placements. Sort by deadline so nothing slips by.

3. Apply and keep score

Follow every application through each stage and collect your certificates and wins in one place.

About CampusConnect

CampusConnect is a career and opportunity platform built for students. Good opportunities are scattered across notice boards, group chats and emails, and they are easy to miss.

We bring them together so every student can find, apply to and track what matters, whatever college or branch they come from.

  • FindOne searchable list for internships, jobs, hackathons, workshops, placements, competitions and events.
  • Stay on timeDeadlines are front and center, so you know what closes soon.
  • Show your progressKeep your applications and achievements together as you grow.
